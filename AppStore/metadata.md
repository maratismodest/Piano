## Description (max 4000)

Piano is a simple, beautiful keyboard for your iPhone and iPad.

Every note is recorded from a real Kawai upright piano, so it sounds like an instrument, not a ringtone.

FEATURES
• Real piano sound — sampled from an acoustic upright piano
• Multi-touch — play chords with as many fingers as you like
• Glissando — slide a finger across the keys to play them in turn
• Zoom — show anywhere from one to two octaves, so keys are as wide as you need
• Shift — move the keyboard one white key at a time, from C1 all the way to C8
• Remembers your zoom and position between launches
• Works offline

NO DISTRACTIONS
No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Open the app and play.
