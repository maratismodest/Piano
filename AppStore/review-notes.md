2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Piano is a simple, ad-free piano keyboard for iPhone and iPad. It is for anyone who wants to play or practice a melody, pick out a tune by ear, check a note or chord, or just play for fun: beginners, students, hobby musicians, and children. It solves the problem that most piano apps are cluttered with ads, subscriptions, and menus; Piano opens straight to a keyboard with a realistic acoustic piano sound.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES
No setup, login, or sample files are required. Turn up the volume and launch the app.
- Play: tap any key. Use several fingers to play chords.
- Glissando: slide a finger across the keys to play them one after another.
- Zoom: use the stepper next to the magnifying glass icon. "+" shows fewer, wider keys (down to one octave), "−" shows more keys (up to two octaves).
- Shift: use the stepper next to the left-right arrow icon to move the keyboard one white key at a time, from C1 up to C8.
- The zoom level and keyboard position are remembered between launches.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. It uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, authentication, payment, or AI services. Sound is produced on-device with Apple's AVFoundation (AVAudioEngine / AVAudioUnitSampler).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions.
