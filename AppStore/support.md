# Piano — Support

Piano is a simple piano keyboard for iPhone and iPad with the sound of a real upright piano.

## How to play

- **Play:** tap the keys. Use several fingers for chords.
- **Glissando:** slide a finger across the keys to play them one after another.
- **Zoom (magnifier):** **+** makes the keys bigger (fewer keys on screen), **−** shows more keys — from one to two octaves.
- **Shift (↔):** **−** / **+** moves the keyboard one white key left or right, from C1 to C8.

Your zoom and position are saved automatically.

## Frequently asked questions

**There's no sound.**
Turn the volume up with the side buttons. If you use Bluetooth headphones, check they are connected. If there's still no sound, close the app completely and open it again.

**Does the app collect any data?**
No. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads, or network access. See the Privacy Policy: https://gist.github.com/maratismodest/07f29653e9d0f0b8bd042c72d108cccc
