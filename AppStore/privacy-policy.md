# Privacy Policy — Piano

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Piano ("the app") is developed by Marat Faizerakhmanov.

## Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party SDKs
- No network requests — the app works entirely offline

The app saves only two display settings, keyboard zoom and position, on your
device so they are restored the next time you open it. These settings never
leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.
